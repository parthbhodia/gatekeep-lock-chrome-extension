## What Cat Break does
Cat Break tracks how long you actively spend on a website and plays a full-screen cat video once you reach a limit you set. Limits, exclusions, and timings are chosen by you and stored on your own device.

## What Cat Break is not
- **Not a security or content-blocking tool.** The break overlay can be dismissed with `Esc` or the "Shoo" button, and the extension can be disabled or removed at any time.
- **Not parental-control or monitoring software.** It does not enforce limits against a user's wishes and does not report activity to anyone.
- **Not medical, psychological, or wellbeing advice.** It is a reminder to take a break, nothing more.

Do not rely on Cat Break in any situation where a missed, late, or bypassed break would cause harm.
