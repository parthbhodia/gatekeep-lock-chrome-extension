How it works

Three steps, then forget about it.

  1. 1

    Pick your limit

    15, 30, 45, 60, 90 minutes — or anything up to 600. One default for everything, and per-site rules when you want them.

  2. 2

    Browse like normal

    The timer only runs while the tab is actually focused. Switch tabs or apps and it pauses. Background tabs never eat your limit.

  3. 3

    The cat arrives

    Full screen, video paused, countdown running. When your break is up it leaves on its own — or shoo it away early.

Honest timing

It only counts the time you were really there.

Most trackers count a tab as "open." Cat Break counts a tab as focused. Alt-tab to your editor, take a call, walk away — the clock stops. The 45 minutes it reports are 45 minutes you actually spent.

  • Pauses when the tab loses focus
  • Pauses when the whole window loses focus
  • Totals reset at midnight

It handles the video

Your autoplay doesn’t get to keep going.

A break you can hear isn’t a break. When the cat lands, Cat Break pauses what’s playing — including players buried in shadow DOM, like YouTube’s. If a page refuses to pause, it mutes the tab instead. When you’re done, playback picks back up.

Paused for your break — resumes after

You stay in control

The break ends itself. No willpower required.

Set how long the cat stays — a 2-minute stretch or a proper 10. A countdown runs in the corner and the overlay clears on its own. Need out early? Press Esc or shoo it from the popup. It’s a nudge, not a cage.

Questions

The usual ones.

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no ads, no paid tier, no upsell. It’s MIT-licensed and the source is on GitHub.

What data do you collect?

The extension collects nothing. Settings and daily totals live in chrome.storage on your own machine — no server, no account, no sync, no analytics. The only thing it fetches is the cat video itself.

This website is separate. catbreak.com uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages people read. That’s the marketing site only — it has no connection to the extension, which never reports your browsing anywhere. Details in the privacy policy.

Does it work on every site?

Any normal http or https page. Chrome’s internal pages (chrome://, the Web Store) are off-limits to every extension, including this one.

Do background tabs count against me?

No. The timer only runs while that tab is the active, focused one. Leave twelve tabs open — only the one you’re looking at counts.

Can I limit just one or two sites?

Yes. Add them under Limit by site and turn on Only limit sites on the list below. Everything else is then ignored completely.

What if I genuinely need to keep working?

Press Esc or hit Shoo in the popup and it’s gone. Cat Break is a speed bump, not a lock. That’s the whole point.

Does the timer reset?

Every day at midnight. After a break finishes, that site’s clock also starts fresh.
